# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

First preview: the PaperHawk shell in Bytehawk's Hawkline style.

- Hawkline sidebar: Open file, search, Home, Viewer, Files, All tools, Edit PDF, Fill & Sign, Organize pages, Combine files, Export PDF, Create PDF, Protect, Scan & OCR, a list of recent documents, light/dark mode and Settings.
- Viewer: an Acrobat-style reader with a toolbar, page thumbnails and bookmarks on the left, comments and document properties on the right, and floating page and zoom controls. Page through, jump to a page, zoom (25% to 400%, fit width, fit page, Ctrl+mouse wheel), one or two pages side by side, rotate the view, and drag around with the hand tool.
- Comments: highlights and sticky notes on the pages, listed in the comments panel. Click one on either side to jump to it.
- Files: every document with filters for recent, starred, shared, waiting for signature, forms and scans.
- All tools: every planned tool, grouped like Acrobat's tool center.
- Home: documents to continue reading, things waiting for you, recent comments and tools.
- Six sample documents (a report, a lease waiting for signatures, a form, a guide, an invoice and a scanned receipt). Opening your own PDFs, editing, signing and the other tools are marked coming soon.
